# Rebuild 6.0 Development Architecture

Status date: 2026-10-03

## Status and boundary

[PROVISIONAL] This document establishes a development framework from HANDOFF/CURRENT_STATE.md. It is not Canon and does not promote proposals by describing them.

[FACT] SOURCE/v5.8/SYNAPSE & STEEL.md is an immutable historical snapshot. Rebuild 6.0 work is separate and must never overwrite or modify it.

[PROPOSAL] Target identity: ONE ENGINE. TWELVE PHILOSOPHIES. ONE LIVING WORLD. The recorded design questions are who or what is entitled to decide humanity’s future, and whether humanity can remain human when it no longer makes every decision itself.

## Shared engine and factions

[PROPOSAL] Use one shared engine and four named resources: LOGIC, BIO-MATTER, ALLOY, QUIET. Their rules, costs, and timing require specification and validation.

[FACT] The seven existing source pools are New Carthage, Omega Foundry, Waste-Walkers, Ascetic Monks, Corporate Hegemony, Scrap-Metal Zealots, and Void-Walkers.

[PROVISIONAL] The handoff carries those pools as the Rebuild baseline, subject to source and terminology review.

[PROPOSAL] The five proposed additional factions are The Architect, Deep-Core Syndicate, Glitch-Weavers, Ironclad Accord, and Bio-Spliced Vanguard. These are not v5.8 content or Canon.

[PROPOSAL] Target: 12 pools × 24 cards = 288 faction cards. Card generation is deferred.

## Agency, authority, information, and ATS

[PROPOSAL] Keep decision method and authority distinct: ATS describes how an autonomous opponent chooses; Warden describes who or what has authority.

[PROVISIONAL] Agency modes: DIRECT, DELEGATED, CONTESTED, AUTONOMOUS. Record: AGENT → AUTHORITY → INFORMATION → LEGAL OPTIONS → PRIORITIES → CHOICE → COUNTERPLAY → RESULT → CONSEQUENCE → HISTORY.

[PROPOSAL] Sovereignty ladder: INFORMATION → CAPABILITY → PERMISSION → AUTHORITY → DELEGATION → AUTONOMY → SOVEREIGNTY.

[PROVISIONAL] Warden levels: 0 NONE, 1 ADVISE, 2 ASSIST, 3 DELEGATE, 4 OPERATE, 5 AUTONOMOUS, 6 SOVEREIGN. Proposed contract form: IF CONDITION → AUTHORIZE ACTION → WITH LIMIT → UNTIL END. Permission does not itself establish intelligence, consciousness, moral status, identity, or competence.

[PROPOSAL] Information categories: PUBLIC, PRIVATE, PERMITTED, INFERRED, PREDICTED, UNKNOWN, FORBIDDEN. Preserve the rule: NO AGENT MAY USE INFORMATION OUTSIDE ITS OBSERVATION RIGHTS.

[PROPOSAL] ATS flow: OBSERVE → FILTER LEGAL ACTIONS → SCORE → APPLY FACTION WEIGHTS → APPLY PERSONALITY → HANDLE UNCERTAINTY → TIEBREAK → CHOOSE → LOG. Evaluation dimensions named by the handoff: Survival, Objective, Denial, Opportunity, Identity, Consequence, Information, Agency, Warden/Authority risk, Tiebreak.

[PROVISIONAL] Difficulty I–V must not secretly change legal rules, hidden information, costs, victory conditions, card text, or resource availability.

## Cards and world systems

[PROPOSAL] Future card fields may include ID, Name, Faction, Type, Subtype, Cost, Timing, Legal Targets, Information Requirements, Choice, Effect, Duration, Counterplay, Consequence, History, Market/Sunder/Warden Interactions, Agency Type, ATS Priority/Tiebreak, Failure Conditions, Theme Question, and Lore Function. This schema is unvalidated; no cards are created here.

[PROVISIONAL] Sunder track: STABLE → STRESSED → SUNDERED → ANOMALOUS → CATASTROPHIC. The handoff describes the battlefield as playable.

[PROPOSAL] Preserve separate history and continuity questions: EVENT → RECORD → HISTORY → REFERENCE → CONSEQUENCE. An Echo is not automatically the entity it represents; reconstruction does not automatically establish subjective continuity.

[PROVISIONAL] Character-value terminology is conflicted between v5.8 and earlier architecture. Preserve both trails; do not silently replace either. Identity dimensions named by the handoff: Subjective, Social, System.

[PROPOSAL] Settlement, authority-site, identity-site, creature, anomaly, and character indexes remain source material for later review, not expanded canon in this document.

## Validation and production gates

[PROPOSAL] Validate through DESIGN → QUANTITATIVE BASELINE → RULES AUDIT → ABUSE TEST → SOLO/AUTOMATED TESTING → HUMAN PLAYTEST → EXPERIENCE REPORTS → REVISE → RETEST → FREEZE.

[PROVISIONAL] Record deterministic version, seed, factions, decks, first player, ATS profile, difficulty, Warden configuration, results, and agency telemetry. Candidate profiles: Random, Greedy, Balanced, Factional, Adversarial, Warden; Search and Learning are future profiles.

[PROPOSAL] Sequence: source and boundary audit; terminology freeze; universal rules; Core and Directive redesign; Warden contract matrix; character interaction model; existing seven pools; five proposed pools; neutral/system cards and Ultimate Threats; deterministic ATS and 66-matchup matrix; human playtesting.

[FACT] This architecture is a framework only. It does not generate 288 cards, change Canon, resolve source conflicts, or authorize a merge or push.
